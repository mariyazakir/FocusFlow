## Information FocusFlow handles

### Information you enter

Tasks may contain titles, notes, categories, priorities, due dates, estimated focus time, status, and recurrence/permanent-task settings. You may also create a local backup file. Do not enter information you would not want stored on your computer or in your browser.

### Local application data

- **Python/Streamlit edition:** task information, permanent-task completion history, and the version/time of your terms acceptance are stored in the local `focusflow.db` SQLite file beside the application. The app does not send task information to a FocusFlow server or cloud database.
- **Installable web edition:** task information, theme preference, and the version/time of your terms acceptance are stored in your browser’s local storage. The service worker caches the static app files so the app shell can work offline after a visit. Task information is not sent to the app operator by the app code and is not synchronized to another device.
- **Backups:** exporting creates a file on your device. Importing reads the file in your browser. You control where you save or share that file.

### Basic connection information for the hosted web edition

When you open the hosted web edition, your browser requests static files from the hosting provider (currently intended to be Vercel). The provider may process connection and request information such as IP address, requested paths, timestamps, browser/device details, and security or diagnostic logs under its own terms and privacy notice. This is separate from task content, which the app stores in your browser. Review [Vercel’s Privacy Notice](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-notice) and the hosting provider’s current documentation. If you change hosts, update this Policy.

The current app code does not include FocusFlow accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or a FocusFlow cookie-based tracking system. A browser or hosting provider may still use its own strictly necessary technologies or logs; check their current notices. No claim here overrides a third party’s own privacy practices.

## Why information is used

Task information is used in your browser/app to display and manage your lists, calendar, board, charts, planner, print view, and backups. The acceptance record is used to remember which policy version you accepted on that installation/browser. Hosting connection information is handled by the host for delivering and securing the site, according to the host’s own terms.

## Sharing and sale

The app code does not transmit task content to the operator or sell task content. You can disclose task information by exporting or sharing a backup, printing it, or allowing someone access to your device/browser or SQLite file. The web host may receive ordinary web requests and associated connection data as described above. No other app-controlled sharing is built into this version.

## Retention, deletion, and control

Local task data remains until you delete it in the app or remove the relevant local storage/database file. The operator cannot remotely retrieve or delete data stored only on your device. Exported backups are separate files and must be deleted separately. Browser data deletion may also clear tasks and consent preferences. The app keeps permanent-task date/status history locally as long as its database remains.

For the hosted site’s request logs, retention and access are controlled by the hosting provider and may depend on its plan and settings; consult its current notice and dashboard. The operator does not control those logs.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability of personal information, and to complain to a regulator. Because FocusFlow does not collect task content on an operator server in this version, you can usually exercise control directly in the app/device. Contact the operator for questions or requests about information the operator may hold. Applicable rights and exceptions depend on local law.

## Security and limits

Local storage means the security of task data depends substantially on your device, browser profile, operating-system account, backups, and anyone with access to them. Local storage is not a substitute for device encryption or a secure backup. The operator cannot promise that any device, browser, network, or hosting service is perfectly secure. Use a protected device and avoid storing sensitive information.

## Children

FocusFlow is not designed to collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information directly to the operator, contact the operator using the details above. Since task data is local in this version, a parent or guardian can remove the app’s local data from that device/browser.
