FocusFlow Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026<br> Operator: Mariya Zakir<br> Contact: mariyazakir.hh@gmail.com<br> Location: Uttar Pradesh, India

This Policy describes the FocusFlow Python/Streamlit application and its separate installable web application. The operator is Mariya Zakir. Read this Policy together with the Terms of Use.

Information FocusFlow handles

Information you enter

Tasks can contain titles, notes, categories, priorities, due dates, estimated focus time, status, and recurrence/permanent-task settings. You may also create a local backup file. Avoid entering passwords, payment details, health information, confidential records, or other sensitive information.

Local application data

  • Python/Streamlit edition: task information and permanent-task completion history are stored in the local focusflow.db SQLite file beside the application. The policy version and acceptance time are also stored locally in that database. The app does not send task information to a FocusFlow server or cloud database.
  • Installable web edition: task information and theme preference are stored in the current browser’s local storage. The policy version and acceptance time are stored there as well. The service worker caches static app files so the app shell can work offline after a visit. App code does not send task content to the operator or synchronize it to another device.
  • Backups: exporting creates a file on your device; importing reads the file in your browser. You control where you save or share that file. Backups contain task data, not a server-side account.

Basic connection information for the hosted edition

When you open the hosted web edition, your browser requests static files from the hosting provider (currently intended to be Vercel). The provider may process connection and request information such as IP address, requested paths, timestamps, browser/device details, and security or diagnostic logs under its own terms and privacy notice. This is separate from task content, which the app stores in your browser. Review Vercel’s Privacy Notice. If the host changes, this Policy should be updated.

The current app code does not include FocusFlow accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or a FocusFlow cookie-based tracking system. A browser or hosting provider may still use its own necessary technologies or logs; check their current notices. This Policy does not override a third party’s own privacy practices.

How information is used and shared

Task information is used on your device to display and manage lists, the calendar, board, charts, planner, print view, and backups. The local consent record remembers which policy version you accepted. Hosting connection information is handled by the host to deliver and secure the site, under the host’s own terms.

The app code does not transmit task content to the operator or sell task content. You may disclose task information by exporting or sharing a backup, printing it, or allowing another person access to your device/browser or SQLite file. The web host receives ordinary web requests and associated connection data as described above. No other app-controlled sharing is built into this version.

Retention, deletion, and control

Local task data remains until you delete it in the app or remove the relevant local storage/database file. The operator cannot remotely retrieve or delete data stored only on your device. Exported backups are separate files and must be deleted separately. Clearing browser data may also clear tasks and the local consent record. Permanent-task history remains in the local database for as long as that database remains.

For the hosted site’s request logs, retention and access are controlled by the hosting provider and may depend on its plan and settings; consult its current notice and dashboard. The operator does not control those logs.

For questions or requests about information the operator may hold, contact mariyazakir.hh@gmail.com. Because task content remains on your device, you can usually manage or delete it directly in the app/device. Rights and exceptions depend on applicable law.

Security and limits

Local storage means task-data security depends substantially on your device, browser profile, operating-system account, backups, and anyone with access to them. Local storage is not a substitute for device encryption or a secure backup. The operator cannot promise that any device, browser, network, or hosting service is perfectly secure.

Children and age eligibility

FocusFlow does not ask users for their age and does not provide age checks or parental/guardian verification. Users of any age can use the app. Task content is stored on the user's device, but a user may choose to enter personal details in task titles or notes; avoid entering identifying or sensitive information, especially for children. The app does not collect a child's age or guardian details, and its first-use policy acknowledgement is not a parental-consent verification system.

Legal requirements for children's personal data depend on where the app is used and may apply even when an app is free or stores content locally. In India, the government has scheduled the main DPDP Act provisions, including section 9 on children's data, to take effect 18 months after the 13 November 2025 commencement notification (13 May 2027). This schedule is not a legal determination that FocusFlow is exempt; the operator must reassess the app and its hosting/data flows before those provisions take effect and wherever else it is offered. Official commencement notification.
